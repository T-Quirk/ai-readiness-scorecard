From your answers, the best next step is a few practical changes your team can make now. Here are three that work in most contractors and consultancies:

  1. Give AI your own past jobs, not a blank page. Before you ask ChatGPT or Claude to price or check a scope, paste in two or three past estimates for similar work. It then works in your rates and your format instead of guessing.
  2. Make it list its assumptions. End every estimating or cost prompt with "list every assumption and every item you left out". Most AI pricing errors come from things it silently assumed or skipped, and this brings them to the surface for your estimator to sign off.
  3. Turn one monthly report into a margin check. Export your job-cost report from your accounts system once a month and ask AI for a one-page summary per job: budget vs actual, costs posted to the wrong job, and anything unbilled. Twenty minutes, and you see margin leaks while the job is still running.
